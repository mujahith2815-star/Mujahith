01 / Introduction

About Me

A developer mindset with an engineering-first approach.

I’m M. Mohammed Mujahith, an Artificial Intelligence and Data Science student at Stella Amrys College of Engineering. I’m interested in building technology, not just studying it. I move between software and hardware: writing Python, exploring AI models, setting up Linux development environments, experimenting with microcontrollers and planning electronic systems.

I like taking an idea apart, understanding its components, and designing a practical path to a working prototype. I’m especially interested in robotics and intelligent devices where software, electronics, mechanics and communication come together.

At this stage, I’m strengthening my fundamentals through personal projects and hands-on experiments. I document concepts, test small subsystems and treat unfinished ideas honestly as research, prototypes or active development — not as completed products.

My long-term ambition is to become a cross-disciplinary engineer who can design intelligent machines and useful hardware products from the system architecture down to the circuit level.
